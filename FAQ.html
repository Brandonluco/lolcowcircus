What's the point of this website?

This website serves as a platform for users to discuss various streamers anonymously. Additionally, it provides an opportunity to submit your own articles about any streamer of your choice.

Can I request streamers?

Yes, please email me any new streamer you believe qualifies as a lolcow. There is no guarantee I will follow them; it depends on whether they are worth covering or not.

How often will articles be posted?

Articles will be posted on no less than a weekly basis.

Can I write my own articles to submit?

Yes, please send an email in this format.

  • Title
  • Date
  • Author (which you would add/use for attribution)(can be anonymous)
  • Top content paragraph(s)
  • Optional image
  • Optional YouTube video
  • Bottom content paragraph(s)(optional)

Articles will be posted once I see the request.

What is not allowed in submitted articles?

I want to be as lenient as possible, but there are some things that simply can’t be posted for obvious reasons.

  • Direct threats (“someone should hurt/kill X”)
  • Calls for people to attack someone
  • Praising or encouraging real-world violence
  • Allegations of criminal conduct must be supported by credible sources. Do not present unverified accusations as fact. Posts making serious allegations may be removed or edited.
  • Opinions are allowed. Unverified criminal accusations are not.
  • Publishing someone's private information
  • Doxxing
  • Impersonation
